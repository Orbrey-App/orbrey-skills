# Routine Builder — Reference

Reference step inventories for common routines. Adjust per member age and household.

---

## Morning (kid, 6–10)

- Wake & toilet
- Get dressed (uniform out night before)
- Breakfast
- Brush teeth
- Pack drink bottle / lunchbox into bag
- Shoes & jacket
- Final check + out

Cap: 7 steps · ~75 min total.

## Morning (kid, 11–14)

- Wake (alarm-managed)
- Shower if needed
- Get dressed
- Breakfast
- Teeth + face
- Pack bag (homework, sport kit, drink)
- Out

Cap: 7 steps · ~60 min.

## Morning (teen, 15+)

Self-managed. Build the routine as a single occurrence: "Out the door by {{HH:mm}}". Resist micro-managing.

## School-prep (after-school, kid)

- Hang up bag, take out lunchbox
- Snack
- 15 min decompression (no screens)
- Homework
- Free play
- Tidy room before dinner

## Bedtime (kid, 6–10)

- Tidy room (5 min reset)
- Lay out tomorrow's uniform
- Pyjamas
- Brush teeth
- Read with adult (10–20 min)
- Lights out

Cap: 6 steps · ~30 min.

## Bedtime (teen)

- Lights out by {{HH:mm}} (single occurrence)
- Optional: homework log check

## Dinner (whole family)

- Set table (kid task — see chore-rotator)
- Eat
- Clear table (kid task)
- Dishwasher load (rotated)
- Lunchboxes prepped for tomorrow (parent or rotated)

## Weekend (loose)

- Family breakfast
- Outdoor / activity slot
- Quiet hour
- Family meal
- Sunday-night reset (uniform, lunchbox, calendar review)

---

## Time Budgeting Defaults

| Step | Typical minutes |
|---|---:|
| Wake & toilet | 5 |
| Shower (kid) | 10 |
| Shower (adult/teen) | 15 |
| Get dressed (laid out) | 5–10 |
| Breakfast (sit-down) | 15–25 |
| Brush teeth | 3–5 |
| Pack bag | 5 |
| Read (bedtime) | 10–20 |
| Tidy room (reset) | 5–10 |
| Family meal (dinner) | 30–45 |

These are realistic averages, not aspirational. Adjust upward for kids who genuinely need longer.
