## Recommended: Halloumi & roast pumpkin bowls

**Servings:** 4 (will need to scale up — 5 diners)
**Total time:** 25 min

### Have on hand
- Halloumi — 400 g (recipe needs 250 g; surplus 150 g — bonus snack)
- Pumpkin (cubed) — 600 g (recipe needs 500 g)
- Olive oil — yes
- Cumin — yes
- Lemons — 4 (recipe needs 1)
- Spinach (baby) — 200 g (recipe needs 150 g)
- Couscous — yes (recipe base)
- Garlic — yes

### Gaps
- **Parsley** — recipe calls for 2 tbsp.
  - Substitute: use coriander (in fridge); flavour profile shifts slightly toward warmer/herby. Acceptable.

### Plan
1. **Scale up by 25%** — recipe is for 4, you have 5 diners. 250→315 g halloumi, 500→625 g pumpkin, 1→1.25 cups couscous.
2. Roast pumpkin first (15 min @ 200°C); cook couscous while halloumi pan-fries (5 min); plate; squeeze lemon; coriander on top.
3. Eli's plate — pull halloumi out before final lemon (he won't eat it lemony).
